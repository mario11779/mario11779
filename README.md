# Mario Costanz

I'm the founder and CEO of [Workforce AI](https://www.hireworkforce.ai). We build AI employees for relationship-driven businesses: agencies, advisory shops, staffing, real estate, insurance, law, accounting. Firms where the owner knows every client by name and the work lives in the inbox and on the phone.

The AI employee joins your Zoom as a talking face, answers questions live, and takes the notes. It drafts your email in your voice and waits for you to approve it. It keeps a file on every client so nothing gets re-explained. We call it AI for the rest of us.

I've been pointed at the future of work for a while. In July 2017, Forbes ran a set of questions entrepreneurs wanted to put to Mark Zuckerberg. Mine was about universal basic income: "In my estimation, blockchain, AI, driverless cars and VR ... will eliminate 30% of the jobs in our country over the next decade." I was running a tax company then. Now I build the software.

The username is my old DSNY badge number. I picked up New York's garbage from 1999 to 2002.

Accounting Today named me One to Watch on its Top 100 Most Influential People in Accounting list in 2017 and again in 2018. I wrote two books on tax and entrepreneurship. My first OpenAI account dates to April 10, 2021.
